## hero-container-ship.jpg

Aerial view of a container ship at sea.

- **Source:** Unsplash, photo `0A7YwYhZhWw`
- **Licence:** [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license) — free to use, including
  commercially, with no permission or attribution required. The credit here is courtesy, not an
  obligation.
- **Why this photograph:** it shows containerised cargo without any legible third-party carrier
  branding. Several otherwise better-composed terminal photographs were rejected because Maersk,
  Evergreen, ONE and OOCL marks were clearly readable in them. SDG Lines is a simulated carrier, and
  putting a real carrier's containers on its home page would suggest an association that does not
  exist and would confuse a learner about what SDG Lines is.
